p. 0201 — Better company

You don’t need more discipline. You need better company.

Whatever moved you today, keep it. REPS holds on to it, and keeps it close — so the page you underlined in your head is still there next month, and the month after.

Then, in the minutes you’d have spent scrolling, there’s something better to open: a short game made of your own reading. It’s play. It’s yours. And it quietly turns what you read into what you know.

p. 0302 — The person who remembers the book

p. 0504 — Why this exists
Mike McGraw
Founder. Builds and uses REPS every day.

A letter from the founder

I spent twelve years investing in technology, and watched my nieces and nephews grow up on YouTube Shorts. Then I read that population-level measures of intelligence have started to fall, for the first time since we began counting.

So I deleted Instagram, went back to books and long documentaries, and on my thirty-fourth birthday I left my job to build this: something that keeps you company with what you’ve chosen to know, and asks nothing of you but a few good minutes.

— Mike
