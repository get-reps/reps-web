Why REPS exists

Mike McGraw, founder

“Orwell feared the people who would ban books. Huxley feared the people who would stop wanting them. Neil Postman wrote it in 1985. The feed is Huxley's.”

Mike spent twelve years investing in technology: CDPQ's tech team, then Inovia Capital, where he opened the European office. On 30 June 2026, his 34th birthday, he left to build REPS full-time. He had watched his nephews and nieces grow up on YouTube Shorts and read that the Flynn effect had reversed. He deleted Instagram in 2025, and he builds and uses REPS every day.
