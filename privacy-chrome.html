Chrome Extension Privacy

Effective: May 11, 2026

This page explains what the REPS Chrome extension collects, what it doesn't, and how the data it does handle is used. It covers the extension specifically — for how REPS as a whole handles your information, see our main Privacy Policy.

Short version: the extension only acts when you explicitly invoke it (toolbar click, right-click menu, or keyboard shortcut). It does not read pages you haven't saved, does not track your browsing, does not run background scripts, and does not include any analytics or third-party trackers.

1. What the Extension Collects

(a) The pairing token

To pair the extension with your REPS account the extension uses a one-time code. There are two flows:

  • Extension-initiated (QR code). When you open the options page the extension calls the REPS backend to create a temporary, anonymous pairing row. A short-lived pre-claim token is stored in chrome.storage.local under the key pendingPairing while you scan the QR code with the REPS mobile app. Once you confirm pairing on your phone, the backend links the token to your account; the extension detects this within a few seconds, promotes the token to the canonical pairing storage key, and clears the temporary pendingPairing entry.
  • App-initiated (typed code). You generate a one-time code in the REPS mobile app and type it into the extension's options page. The extension sends the code to the REPS backend, which issues the device token and links it to your account.

In both cases the resulting device token is an opaque identifier tied to your account. It is stored in chrome.storage.local under the key pairing so the extension stays signed in across browser restarts. It is never shared beyond the REPS ingest pipeline and never used to identify you to third parties.

(b) URLs and page titles you explicitly save

When you click the extension icon, press the keyboard shortcut, or otherwise trigger a "save" action on a page, the extension reads the URL and title of that tab and sends them to your REPS account. No other pages, tabs, or browsing activity are collected.

(c) Text selections you explicitly save

When you highlight text on a page and choose "Save selection to REPS" from the right-click menu, the highlighted excerpt is sent to your REPS account along with the source URL (so you can see where it came from later). Only the selected text is captured — not the rest of the page.

2. What the Extension Does NOT Collect

  • Your browsing history. The extension has no visibility into pages you visit unless you explicitly save them.
  • Page content you haven't saved. The extension never reads or scrapes page bodies in the background. When you save a URL, the extension sends only the URL and title — REPS's server-side pipeline fetches the page content via trusted extraction providers.
  • Keystrokes, mouse movements, or form data. The extension does not observe user activity at all.
  • Analytics or telemetry. The extension does not include Google Analytics, PostHog, Mixpanel, error-reporting SDKs, or any other third-party measurement tools.
  • Personal information. Name, email, age, location, payment information — none of this is requested, read, or collected by the extension. Your name and email live in your paired REPS account and are governed by our main Privacy Policy.

3. How Saved Content Is Used

The URLs, page titles, and text selections you save are sent over HTTPS to the REPS ingest service (a Supabase Edge Function on vciosaulrfvddcenblmo.supabase.co) and stored in your REPS account. From there, they go through the same spaced-repetition processing pipeline as anything you add through the mobile app: extraction (for URLs and videos), concept generation, and rep creation.

Saved content is used solely to power your learning experience inside your own REPS account. It is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used to train AI models outside the normal processing pipeline described in the main Privacy Policy.

4. Where Data Is Stored

Saved content and the device token's hashed form are stored in REPS's backend database (Supabase). REPS and its service providers operate in the United States and other countries; by using the extension you consent to the cross-border transfer of the content you save, under the same terms as our main Privacy Policy.

Retention follows the same rules as your REPS account. When you remove content in the REPS app, it is archived per the main policy's section on archived content. If you request account deletion (see "Your Control" below), the content you've saved through the extension is deleted or anonymised alongside the rest of your account data.

5. Third Parties

Content you save through the extension flows through the same service providers described in the main Privacy Policy. Relevant to the extension:

  • Supabase — hosts your REPS account database. Used by the extension during setup to create and validate pairing codes, and to send saved content to your account. Specific calls: create_anonymous_pairing (creates an anonymous pairing row when you open the options page), validate_device_token (polls every few seconds until pairing is confirmed), and redeem_pairing_code (app-initiated pairing path).
  • n8n — orchestrates REPS's content processing pipeline (the ingest endpoint the extension POSTs to).
  • Content extraction providers (such as Firecrawl and Jina) — fetch the text of URLs you save so they can be processed into learning content. Only the URL is sent to these providers.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — generates concepts and reps from the saved content. Anthropic does not use API inputs to train their models.

The extension does not communicate with any advertising networks, analytics platforms, or other third parties beyond the REPS infrastructure listed above.

6. Your Control

  • Disconnect this browser from local access by uninstalling the extension from chrome://extensions or by clearing the extension's local storage. This removes the device token from this browser.
  • Server-side token revocation is available in the REPS app: open Settings → Connected browsers and disconnect the browser. This centrally invalidates the pairing so that browser can no longer save to your account, whether or not you still have access to it.
  • Archive content you've saved inside the REPS mobile app — the same archive controls apply to content from any ingest source, including the Chrome extension. Archived content is excluded from your training sessions and is no longer visible to you. For permanent deletion of specific content, contact privacy@getreps.io.
  • Request account deletion by emailing privacy@getreps.io, as described in our main Privacy Policy. We will delete or anonymise your personal information within 30 days of your request, including everything you've saved through the extension.

7. Remote Code

The extension executes no remote code. All JavaScript is bundled into the extension at build time and ships inside the Chrome Web Store package. There are no runtime imports, no eval calls, and no dynamic script loading from any origin. Every response the extension receives over the network is treated as data, not code.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Chrome Extension Privacy page as the extension adds capabilities. Material changes will be reflected in the effective date at the top of the page and announced through the main REPS Privacy Policy. Continued use of the extension after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
