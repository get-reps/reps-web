How it works

Four steps. Under two minutes.

1

Install the extension.

One click in the Chrome Web Store. The REPS icon appears in your browser bar.
2

Pin it to your toolbar.

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome, find REPS, and hit the pin. Otherwise it hides behind the puzzle menu and you'll forget it's there.
3

Pair with your account.

Scan the QR with your phone. REPS opens, the browser pairs, and you're done — one time, about ten seconds.
4

Save with the shortcut.

Hit the keyboard shortcut to save the whole page — or highlight a passage first and the same shortcut saves just that selection. Articles, YouTube videos, podcast pages — they land in your REPS as fresh reps before your next training session.

Ctrl + Shift + S or ⌘ + Shift + S on Mac

Prefer clicking? The REPS icon in your toolbar does the same thing.
