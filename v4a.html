Why REPS exists

We have never taken in more and kept less.

The feed is built to be forgotten: scroll, swipe, gone. REPS runs the other way. It is a free iPhone app that turns what you read, watch and listen to into a short daily game made from your own material, so the things you chose to learn stay with you.

How it works

You save it. REPS keeps it company.

Save it from wherever it finds you.

The article in Safari, the episode in Spotify or Apple Podcasts, the video someone sent you. Tap Share, pick REPS, done. Or type the title of the book on your nightstand.

Saving from Safari, YouTube, Spotify, Instagram and Apple Podcasts.

You choose what’s worth keeping.

REPS reads it with you and lays out the ideas inside. You pick the ones that matter to you. Nobody else’s highlights, nobody else’s summary.

Picking the ideas worth keeping from a page on Alexander the Great.

It comes back as a game.

A few minutes a day: a round built from your own material, returning over days and weeks, just as it would start to slip.

Guess the day’s quote in Quotle. Or play with a friend in Friends Trivia: the same ten, side by side.

And it adds up.

Months later, the books you finished are still there, with the ideas you kept from each one. When the conversation turns to one of them, you have something to say.
