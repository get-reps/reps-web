## What REPS does

- **In one line:** An iPhone app that turns what you read, watch and listen to into short quizzes built from that material, plus daily word games, and brings them back over the following days and weeks so you remember it. More: [Home](https://www.getreps.io/)
- **The daily session:** A short daily session, played as a game, made of what is due that day. Four formats: multiple choice, true or false, fill in the blank, and answering from memory. The timing uses spaced repetition. More: [Active recall](https://www.getreps.io/features/active-recall)
- **Progress:** Each idea REPS pulls from what you save moves through six levels, from New to Locked In. A wrong answer brings it back sooner; it never loses a level.
- **Games:** Five games. Ladder, Crossword, Cryptic and Friends Trivia open once three of your saves are ready, and the first three are built from what you save. Quotle, a daily quote game, is open from day one. In Friends Trivia, you and a friend play the same ten rounds and compare scores.
- **Your Brain:** An interactive map of everything you have saved, coloured by topic, that you can explore. More: [Your Brain](https://www.getreps.io/features/second-brain)
- **Ask Echo:** A chat with Echo, the REPS guide, about what you have saved. Echo can also recommend and add sets other people have published.
- **Friends:** Add friends, see their recent activity, play Friends Trivia together and compare progress on a leaderboard.
- **Voice sessions:** Spoken sessions in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese. The app's screens are in English.

## What you can save

- **Sources:** Web articles and links, YouTube videos, Instagram and TikTok links, podcast episode links (Spotify, Apple Podcasts or a feed), PDFs, Word, Pages, Markdown and text documents, photos and screenshots, a photo of a book page, a reading list (a screenshot, a pasted list or a Goodreads export), and your own notes.
- **Ways to save:** In the app, from the iPhone share sheet (a link, text or a single image), with the REPS Chrome extension, or by texting a link or note to Echo over iMessage or emailing it, once connected in the app's settings.

## Where it works, and what it does not do

- **Platform:** iPhone, from the App Store. More: [REPS on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759216018)
- **Not available:** There is no Android app and no web app: you cannot train in a browser. REPS does not work offline.
- **No automatic sync:** REPS does not connect to or import from Notion, Readwise, Kindle, Obsidian, Apple Books or Pocket. You save each item yourself, or import a reading list from a screenshot or a Goodreads export.
- **Who it is for:** Individuals. There are no team, classroom or company accounts, and no ready-made courses: you bring your own content.
