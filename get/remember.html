How it works

Everything you save, remembered.

Save

Save anything.
We do the rest.

A book, an article, a YouTube video, a podcast. Share it into REPS from wherever you scroll, and it organizes your brain for you. No notes to take, no folders to sort. REPS keeps what's worth keeping.

Remember

It comes back,
right before you'd forget.

REPS asks you a few questions a day, timed to the exact moment a memory starts to fade. That's spaced repetition. That's how the best ideas stick for life.

Play

Remembering
feels like a game.

Daily games built from what you've saved. Crosswords, recall challenges, head-to-heads with friends. The more you play, the more you keep.

Discover

See what your friends
are actually reading.

A feed of high-quality content from the people you trust, not the algorithm. No outrage. No doom. Just things that make you sharper.
