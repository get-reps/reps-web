One sentence

REPS is a knowledge companion. It holds what you read, watch, and listen to, so it stays with you instead of fading into a notes app or a camera roll.

A paragraph

REPS is a knowledge companion for people who consume high quality content. You save from the apps you already use. REPS draws out the ideas and holds them in one library that is yours. You can spend a few minutes a day with what you saved, ask questions of it, and see how it connects. Mike McGraw comes from a family of entrepreneurs. He spent 12 years in finance, investing more than a billion dollars across technology and private equity, and turned down a partner promotion to go full-time on it in June 2026. It is on the App Store for iPhone.

Founder, in brief

Mike McGraw comes from a family of entrepreneurs. He spent 12 years in finance, investing more than a billion dollars across technology and private equity, first helping launch PSP's natural resources investment team, then CDPQ's technology investment team, and finally at Inovia Capital, where he opened the firm's European office. Both of his parents are founders: his mother published a wellness magazine, and his father started a graph database company. REPS is where those two meet. In 2025 he started building with AI in his spare hours. On 30 June 2026 he left to work on REPS full-time.
