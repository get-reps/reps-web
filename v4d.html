What it is

REPS is a free iPhone app that keeps what you read, watch and listen to, and turns it into a short daily game made from your own material.

Why now

We have never taken in more and kept less. The feed is built to be forgotten; REPS runs the other way.

Who

Mike McGraw, founder. Twelve years in tech investing; left on his 34th birthday to build this.

How it works

Save it once. It keeps coming back.

Not a course, not a reading list you feel guilty about. Just the things you chose, returning until they’re yours.

Today

Save it from anywhere.

Tap Share in Safari, YouTube, Spotify, Instagram or Apple Podcasts. Add a PDF, a screenshot, a note, a photo of your bookshelf, or just type a book’s title. Saved in seconds.

Shared from Safari, YouTube, Spotify, Instagram and Apple Podcasts

A minute later

Keep the ideas worth keeping.

REPS reads it with you and lays out the ideas inside. You choose what’s worth carrying forward. Nothing becomes yours unless you pick it.

Choosing what to keep from a page on Alexander the Great

Tomorrow, and after

It comes back as a game.

A short daily game built from your own saves, returning over days and weeks just as it starts to fade. Play the Crossword, Ladder, Cryptic or Quotle on your own, or Friends Trivia with a friend: the same round for both of you, then compare scores.

A round of Friends Trivia

Weeks from now

And then it’s simply yours.

Every book, episode and article you kept, connected in one place you can explore, search and play again. Ask Echo about any of it.

Your Knowledge

The one at dinner who remembers the book.

Into Thin Air, the storm on the mountain and the choices made in it. That episode on conversation from the drive to work. The page about Alexander you fell into at midnight.

One by one, they stop being things you once read and start being things you know.

Why REPS exists

I spent twelve years investing in technology, first helping launch CDPQ’s tech team, then at Inovia Capital, where I opened the European office. Then I watched my nephews and nieces grow up on YouTube Shorts, and read that the Flynn effect has reversed.

I think Huxley, not Orwell, called it: control through entertainment that asks nothing of you. I deleted Instagram and went back to books, long reads and documentaries. On 30 June 2026, my 34th birthday, I left to build REPS full-time.

I’m building the antidote.

Mike McGraw, founder
