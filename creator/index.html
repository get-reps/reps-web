Who it’s for

The three people we’re building for.

Strongest pull so far is 40 to 65 — people who read a lot and can feel it starting to slip. They identify as the knowledgeable one in the room and want to stay that way. It works younger too, and we’re testing that.

Sam
Lifelong learner

Retired, with time on their hands and a book club. Self-proclaimed nerd, already plays NYT Games. Cares about staying sharp as the years go by.

Charlie
Curious professional

Lived the before and after of the smartphone, and noticed what it took. Eclectic consumption, no single place for it all. Loves to share discoveries in real life.

Jordan
Informed young adult

Doesn’t trust the feed, but lacks a social alternative. Wants friends around real interests, not algorithms. Seeks an AI that helps, not replaces their thinking.

How we work together

The rhythm

  • Test on Instagram and TikTok first. Fast, cheap, and where trends surface.
  • Recycle what breaks out to Facebook and YouTube, where the core audience lives.
  • One note back per video, usually the next morning. One note, not a list.
  • Under a few hundred views? It’s the hook — we change that first.

What we need from you

  • Honest reactions early. If a flow is slow or an angle feels fake, say it before you film.
  • The product can move. Bad content can’t be fixed in the edit — the mechanics are flexible, the concepts are settled.
  • Please don’t call it a study app or flashcards. Each one drops it into a category we lose in.
