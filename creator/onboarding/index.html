Step 4 of 5 · Learn REPS

The lines to use.

Supercharge your memory.The promise. Lead with it for people who don’t know us yet.
Save anything. Remember everything.The two-beat. Great as a closer or a call to action.
Save. Play. Remember.The loop. Use it to explain how REPS works in three beats.
Stop doomscrolling. Start bloom scrolling.The enemy line. For the manifesto-style, rally-the-viewer videos.
Bring your own content.The difference. Your material, not someone else’s course.

Say

  • “Games” and “daily challenges”
  • Save, remember, keep
  • “Built from your own reading”
  • Brain rot as the enemy

Skip

  • “Study app”, “flashcards”, “quiz”
  • “It’s the X for Y” comparisons
  • “Make it stick”
  • Blaming the viewer. Aim it at the problem.

Step 5 of 5 · Happy REPPING

How we work together.

The rhythm

  • Test on Instagram and TikTok first. Fast, cheap, and where trends surface.
  • Feedback comes at your weekly check-in. One clear note on what to change, not a list.
  • Under a few hundred views? It’s the hook. We change that first.

What we need from you

  • Honest reactions early. If a flow is slow or an angle feels fake, say it before you film.
  • The product can move. Bad content can’t be fixed in the edit. The mechanics are flexible, the concepts are settled.
  • Games and challenges only. Never call REPS a study app, flashcards or a quiz.
  • Turn on the paid-partnership label on every post, and keep your link in your bio.
