# REPS · Learn something. Grow something.

Source: https://www.getreps.io/planet

Why we do this

## Growing your mind should grow something real

REPS is a knowledge companion. What you read, watch, and listen to stays with you. It felt right that it should leave something behind in the world too.

Every time you save something, a little AI reads it and draws out the ideas. That takes a small amount of energy. We could ignore it. Honestly, it is tiny. Instead we turn it into something good. We plant real trees, in real forests, for the people who use REPS.

What we actually do

## Real trees. Every month. Counted.

Every month we plant real, countable trees with [JUST ONE Tree](https://www.justonetree.life/), based on how much REPS was used that month. One pound plants one tree. Real trees in the ground, not a vague promise, and we can point to them. We have already started.

1–3×

More than we use, in the early years

A young tree is only getting started, so it soaks up carbon slowly. Even so, what we plant each month absorbs a few times more than REPS's AI produces.

~100×

More than we use, over a tree's life

As those trees grow, they pull out far more. Across everything we plant, that comes to roughly a hundred times more carbon than we produce.

Mangroves & water

## We help bring water back, too

A big part of what drew us to [JUST ONE Tree](https://www.justonetree.life/) is mangroves. They plant them on coastlines that have lost them, and they help regenerate the oceans around them.

Mangroves do more than almost any forest. They lock away carbon faster, they shield the coast from storms, and they bring water, fish, and life back to places that had lost them. A tree planted here helps bring a stretch of coastline back to life.
