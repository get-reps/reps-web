The AI that powers REPS uses a little energy. So for every month it runs, we plant real trees for it, far more than we ever use.

Why we do this

Growing your mind should grow something real

REPS exists to turn scrolling into something worth keeping. It felt right that it should leave something behind in the world too.

Every time you save something, a little AI reads it, pulls out the ideas worth remembering, and turns them into your daily games. That takes a small amount of energy. We could ignore it, honestly it is tiny. Instead we turn it into something good. We plant real trees, in real forests, for the people who use REPS.

What we actually do

Real trees. Every month. Counted.

Every month we plant real, countable trees with JUST ONE Tree, based on how much REPS was used that month. One pound plants one tree. Real trees in the ground, not a vague promise, and we can point to them. We have already started.

1–3×
More than we use, in the early years
A young tree is only getting started, so it soaks up carbon slowly. Even so, what we plant each month absorbs a few times more than REPS's AI produces.
~100×
More than we use, over a tree's life
As those trees grow, they pull out far more. Across everything we plant, that comes to roughly a hundred times more carbon than we produce.
Mangroves & water

We help bring water back, too

A big part of what drew us to JUST ONE Tree is mangroves. They plant them on coastlines that have lost them, and they help regenerate the oceans around them.

Mangroves do more than almost any forest. They lock away carbon faster, they shield the coast from storms, and they bring water, fish, and life back to places that had lost them. A tree planted here helps bring a stretch of coastline back to life.
