Second Brain

Every concept you've ever saved, in one place.

Your Second Brain is a living map of everything you've trained. Concepts cluster by topic, glow when they're due for review, and bloom as you lock them in. Time-travel through your Second Brain with Chronoscope to see how it grew week by week.

Meet the new Echo

Echo is now a node-sprite.

Echo has been redesigned. The character and the product are now the same thing — a glowing knowledge-graph node, awakened. Same encouraging companion, fully reborn for the Second Brain era.

New app icon
A side-profile brain rendered as a glowing node-and-link network. The icon now mirrors your Second Brain inside the app.
Podcasts

Podcasts now turn into reps.

Paste any podcast link into REPS — the episode becomes a set of reps you can train, just like an article or a video. The thing you listened to on the train doesn't fade by next week.

Send to friends

Send articles and challenges to friends.

Found a great article? Send it straight to a friend's REPS — they'll get the reps in their training queue. Just crushed a Crossword? Challenge a friend to beat your score, right from the result screen.

Chrome extension

Save anything from the web — any day now.

The REPS Chrome extension is in final review with the Chrome Web Store. Once approved (any day now), you'll be able to highlight any passage, right-click, and send it straight to your Second Brain.
