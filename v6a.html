Why REPS exists

A note from Mike.

I spent twelve years investing in technology, and watched my nephews and nieces grow up on YouTube Shorts. Neil Postman saw it coming in 1985: it was Huxley, not Orwell, who got it right. The danger isn't what we're forbidden. It's entertainment that asks nothing of us.

I deleted Instagram, went back to books and long reads, and left investing on my 34th birthday to build the thing I wanted to exist. I use it every day. I'm building the antidote.
