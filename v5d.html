Why it exists

Twelve years in technology. One decision.

I spent twelve years investing in technology and watched my nephews and nieces grow up on Shorts. Then I read that the long rise in measured intelligence has, for the first time, turned around. Neil Postman wrote it in 1985: Huxley, not Orwell, called it — the danger isn't what we're forced to watch, it's what we'd happily never stop watching. On my 34th birthday I left investing to build the other thing.
