REPS vs Recall: two ways to keep what you learn

By Mike McGraw, founder of REPS · Updated November 2026 · 5 min read
We make REPS, so we are not neutral.

REPS is an AI companion for iPhone: you save an article, a video, a podcast or a book page, tap the ideas you want to remember, and they come back as a short daily game, with word games and trivia with friends. Recall (recall.it) is the broader tool: an AI knowledge base that saves, summarizes and connects what you read, watch and hear, on the web, iOS, Android and four browsers, with chat and quizzes on its paid Plus plan. Choose REPS to remember what you save, Recall to research and organize it.

Recall facts are from recall.it, its pricing page, quiz docs and App Store listing; REPS facts are from our facts page. Checked 24 November 2026. Features and prices change, so confirm current plans at recall.it/pricing.

How each app brings things back

In Recall, you save a card and get a summary. To be quizzed, on the Plus plan, you open the card's Quiz tab, generate questions and can switch on "Schedule for review". A review session gives you up to 10 of your most-due questions, in seven formats including matching, ordering and flashcards (Recall docs).

In REPS, you save the thing and REPS pulls out the ideas worth keeping. You tap the ones you want to remember, now or later, and they come back in a short daily session played as a game, in four formats: multiple choice, true or false, fill in the blank, and answering from memory. Each idea moves through six levels, from New to Locked In; a wrong answer brings it back sooner but never costs a level (REPS facts). In Recall, a wrong answer on a mastered question drops it back to Learning. Outside the daily session, the Discover feed lists new articles to explore, each showing how many concepts it holds.

Where REPS fits better

  • You want a few minutes a day of questions played as a game, rather than a library to manage.
  • You want word games made from your saves: Ladder, Crossword and Cryptic, plus Quotle, a daily quote game.
  • You want friends in it: see their activity, play Friends Trivia on the same ten rounds and compare progress on a leaderboard.
  • You want to practise out loud with Echo, the REPS guide, in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian or Portuguese.
  • You save books: a photo of a page, or a reading list from a screenshot or a Goodreads export.

Where Recall fits better

  • You need Android, the web, or an extension for Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari (Recall).
  • You want AI summaries and chat across your library or the web (Plus), with your choice of AI model, including ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini (Max) (pricing).
  • You are building a research library: a notes editor, a knowledge graph, imports (bulk on Plus) and Markdown export.
  • You want API and MCP access, which is included even on the free plan (pricing).
  • You want PDFs up to 2,000 pages, EPUBs, Google Docs and Slides as sources (supported content).

Where REPS is weaker

  • iPhone only: no Android app, no web app, and no offline use.
  • No library imports from other apps, only a reading list from a screenshot or a Goodreads export.
  • AI summaries are not the focus, and Ask Echo covers what you have saved, not the open web.
  • Four question formats to Recall's seven, and less you can configure.
FAQ

Frequently asked

Is REPS the same as Recall?

No. They are separate apps from separate companies. Recall (recall.it, formerly getrecall.ai) is an AI knowledge base made by a team in Amsterdam. REPS is an iPhone app made by REPS AI Ltd in England and Wales. Recall is also not Microsoft's Recall feature in Windows 11, or Recl (recl.app).

Does Recall have spaced repetition?

Yes, on its paid Plus plan. Recall can generate quiz questions from a saved card in seven formats and, if you choose, schedule them on a spaced repetition cycle with five stages from New to Mastered.

Does REPS work on Android, the web or a computer?

No. REPS is an iPhone app from the App Store, with no Android app or web app, and it does not work offline. You can save pages from a computer with the REPS Chrome extension and play on your iPhone.

Can I move my library from Recall to REPS?

Not automatically. Recall exports your data as zipped Markdown files, but REPS does not import your library from other apps (only a reading list from a screenshot or a Goodreads export). You would save the items you care about into REPS one by one.

Is Recall free?

Recall has a free plan with 10 AI summaries a month, unlimited saves and notes, and API and MCP access. Quizzes, chat, Listen Mode and the automatic knowledge graph are on the paid Plus plan, and choosing the AI model is on Max. REPS is on the App Store.
