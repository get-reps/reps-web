# REPS vs Recl: a game you play, or summaries you revisit

![Mike McGraw, founder of REPS](https://www.getreps.io/compare/assets/mike-mcgraw.jpg) **By Mike McGraw**, founder of REPS · Updated November 2026 · 5 min read We make REPS, so we are not neutral.

REPS is an AI companion for iPhone: you save a video, an article, a podcast, a PDF or a book page, tap the ideas you want to keep, and they come back as questions in a short daily game, alongside word games and trivia with friends. Recl (recl.app) summarizes the videos, reels, posts and articles you save, brings each save back after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days and writes a weekly quiz, on iPhone, iPad, Android, the web, Chrome and Edge. Choose REPS to be tested until it stays, Recl to skim and revisit.

## Being asked versus being shown

When a Recl save comes due, you see its key points again and one tap on "Mark reviewed" moves it along ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6804185109)). Separately, Recl writes a weekly quiz from your saves. In REPS, the ideas you tap to keep come back as questions every day: multiple choice, true or false, fill in the blank, and answering from memory. Each idea moves through six levels, from New to Locked In, and a wrong answer brings it back sooner without losing a level ([REPS facts](https://www.getreps.io/facts)). Outside the daily session, the Discover feed lists new articles to explore, each showing how many concepts it holds.

Recl is young: its team announced it was live on iPhone, Android and the web on 3 November 2026 ([launch post](https://x.com/recl_app/status/2117663102296551511)). It is not Recall (recall.it), a separate AI knowledge base; see [REPS vs Recall](https://www.getreps.io/compare/reps-vs-recall).

## Where REPS fits better

- You want to be asked about what you saved every day, not just shown it again.
- You save books (a photo of a page, a reading list from a screenshot or a Goodreads export) and documents such as PDFs, as well as videos and articles.
- You want word games made from your saves (Ladder, Crossword, Cryptic) and Quotle, a daily quote game.
- You want friends in the loop: their activity, Friends Trivia on the same ten rounds and a leaderboard.
- You want spoken sessions with Echo in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian or Portuguese.

## Where Recl fits better

- You need Android, iPad, the web, or an Edge extension.
- You mostly learn from YouTube, reels and TikTok and want a written summary, with chapter timestamps and three summary lengths for YouTube ([App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6804185109)).
- You want to ask your library questions and see which saves the answer used, or connect it to ChatGPT, Claude and Cursor through its MCP server ([Recl](https://www.recl.app/llms.txt)).
- You want the app in one of 20 languages, or to save reels from your Instagram DMs (Pro).
- You like organizing: topics that sort themselves, folders, highlights, notes and a knowledge graph.

## Where REPS is weaker

- The app is iPhone only, with a Chrome extension for saving: no Android app, no web app, and no offline use.
- No library imports from other apps, only a reading list from a screenshot or a Goodreads export.
- Screens are in English; only the spoken sessions come in six languages.

FAQ

## Frequently asked

### Is Recl the same as Recall?

No. Recl (recl.app) and Recall (recall.it, formerly getrecall.ai) are separate apps from different teams. Recl says so itself on its site. We compare REPS with Recall on a separate page.

### Does Recl quiz you?

Yes, in two ways. Each save comes back for review after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days, and one tap marks it reviewed. Separately, Recl writes a weekly quiz from what you saved and sends it as a notification. REPS quizzes you in a short daily session built from the ideas you pick from your saves.

### Which is better for YouTube videos and reels?

If you mainly want a written summary of a video, with timestamps and a choice of length, Recl is built for that. If you want to be tested on what a video said until you remember it, REPS is built for that. Both save YouTube, Instagram and TikTok links.

### Does REPS work on Android or the web?

No. REPS is an iPhone app with a Chrome extension for saving. There is no Android app or web app, and it does not work offline. Recl is on iPhone, iPad, Android, the web, and Chrome and Edge, so it is the better choice if you need Android, iPad or the web.

### Can I try them for free?

Recl is free to try on iPhone, iPad and Android with 3 saves and 5 Ask answers, and needs a paid plan on the web. REPS is currently free on the App Store.
