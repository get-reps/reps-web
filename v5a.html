Why this exists

Mike McGraw, founder. Twelve years investing in technology; left on his 34th birthday to build REPS.

Orwell feared the book would be banned. Huxley feared no one would want to read one. Neil Postman wrote that in 1985, and I think the feed is proving him right: it gives you everything and keeps none of it for you.

I watched my nephews and nieces grow up on Shorts, deleted Instagram, went back to books and long reads, and built the thing I wanted to exist. I’m building the antidote.
